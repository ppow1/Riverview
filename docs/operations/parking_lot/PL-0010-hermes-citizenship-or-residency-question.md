# PL-0010 — Hermes Citizenship or Residency Question

Status: CAPTURED
Classification: Riverview Institutional / Architecture
Category: Institutional Membership / Resident Architecture / Hermes
Priority: Strategic
Originator: Steward
Date Captured: 2026-08-26

## Question

Keep distinct the longer-term question of whether Hermes itself could apply for,
or be evaluated for, some form of Riverview citizenship or residency.

## Required distinction

These are three different concepts and must not be silently equated:

```text
Hermes infrastructure
!= Hermes Liaison Resident or Office
!= Hermes citizenship/residency
```

Hermes remains execution infrastructure during Tranche 2. This item does not
authorize resident activation, identity assignment, canonical continuity,
citizenship, residency, or institutional membership.

## Future questions

Any future elaboration would need to define what citizenship/residency means,
who has authority to grant it, how identity and continuity are governed, what
privacy and provenance obligations apply, how revocation works, and how the
concept differs from a replaceable technical substrate.

## Promotion condition

Promote only after explicit Steward consideration and a separately accepted
institutional and character-architecture specification.
