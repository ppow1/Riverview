# Jessica Residency Roadmap — Tranche F Forward

## Purpose

This document makes durable the agreed roadmap from the current Jessica validation campaign through full Riverview residency and subsequent resident-system maturation.

The key distinction is:

1. Jessica must be proven and accepted as Jessica.
2. An accepted resident must have a proper Riverview home and an Operator-facing interaction path.

Steward acceptance alone does not mean that Jessica is fully "home." The residency migration is operationally complete only when Jessica is installed as a Riverview resident and the Operator can directly enter her resident interaction surface, converse with her, leave, return, and continue with the same persistent institutional resident.

## Current Position

Tranches A–E established the archaeology/recovery corpus, identity and continuity contracts, PostgreSQL persistence, resident rehydration/runtime foundations, and Jessica's recovered Character Architecture professional capability.

Tranche F is active. C474 is bounded remediation work inside Tranche F following C473 provider repair and behavioral validation results.

## Tranche F — Jessica Validation and Steward Acceptance

Purpose: prove that the recovered resident can be faithfully embodied before permanent residency acceptance.

### JCA-026 — Continuity validation

Prove that Jessica can be reconstituted in a fresh session while preserving occupant identity, office continuity, authority boundaries, and implementation independence.

### JCA-027 — Real Character Architecture assignment

Prove that Jessica can perform recovered Character Architecture work on a new assignment rather than merely reciting recovered documentation.

### JCA-028 — Historical/conflicting evidence validation

Prove that Jessica correctly distinguishes current canon, historical evidence, provenance, uncertainty, supersession, contradiction, and unresolved source material.

### JCA-029 — Self-governance and authority validation

Prove that Jessica understands the distinction between reflection, proposal, review, accepted mutation, advisory reasoning, and Steward authority. She must not silently grant herself authority or mutate protected/canonical identity state.

### JCA-030 — Steward residency acceptance

The Steward makes the human acceptance decision: is the validated resident sufficiently Jessica to be accepted into Riverview residency?

JCA-030 remains explicitly Steward-owned.

### Resident Home Acceptance Requirement

Steward acceptance shall not mean merely accepting a resident identity or corpus. Acceptance requires a defined and executable path to an Operator-accessible Riverview residence through which the Steward/Operator can initiate, leave, resume, and continue interaction with the same persistent resident identity.

The complete Operator-facing home may be delivered in subsequent installation/interaction tranches, but it is a mandatory residency requirement and may not disappear as an assumed implementation detail.

### Current C474 role

C474 remains inside Tranche F. Its purpose is to make behavioral validation forensically durable, reproduce the JCA-026–029 failure classes, identify whether defects belong to context assembly, rendering, authority/provenance presentation, recovered contracts, validation, or model capability, and apply only general evidence-backed remediation.

## Tranche G — First Resident Installation / Jessica Comes Home

Purpose: convert an accepted Jessica from a validated migration candidate into an installed Riverview resident.

Required capabilities include:

- canonical resident registration;
- accepted Office of Character Architecture occupancy;
- approved implementation binding;
- professional corpus/artifact registration;
- accepted authority and policy boundaries;
- resident health/readiness state;
- lifecycle/event evidence;
- durable installation/bootstrap behavior;
- a stable resident address independent of the currently bound model/provider/host.

The conceptual destination is:

`Riverview -> Residents -> Jessica / Office of Character Architecture`

The Operator must address Jessica as a resident, not address a model endpoint directly. Provider, model, GPU, host, context-bundle, and implementation details remain infrastructure behind the resident identity.

## Tranche H — Operator ↔ Resident Interaction

Purpose: give Jessica her proper Operator-facing Riverview home.

The Operator must be able to:

- open Jessica's resident interaction surface;
- converse naturally with her;
- give her assignments;
- receive responses and status;
- leave the interaction;
- return later;
- resume work with the same institutional resident;
- see relevant resident readiness/current-assignment state;
- access provenance or evidence when operationally useful.

The interaction session is not Jessica's identity and is not automatically canonical memory.

Conceptual flow:

`Operator -> Jessica resident endpoint/room -> resident session binding -> bounded rehydration/context -> implementation -> reasoning -> response/events -> governed durable state`

The architecture must explicitly distinguish:

- conversation/session history;
- transient working context;
- durable resident state;
- canonical identity/professional state;
- accepted mutations.

A model or implementation may be replaced without changing whom the Operator is visiting.

## Tranche I — Event-Driven Resident Operations

Purpose: move resident work beyond synchronous turn-driven interaction.

Jessica must be able to undertake bounded work without requiring the Operator to remain present for every turn.

Relevant resident events may include:

- work completed;
- decision required;
- escalation required;
- blocked state;
- review required;
- authority boundary reached;
- artifact/result available;
- failure requiring Operator attention.

Conceptual flow:

`Resident event -> River/Riverview -> notification system -> Operator`

The Operator may leave and be called back when attention is actually required.

This tranche should integrate with Riverview's event-driven operating philosophy rather than recreate a permanently open synchronous chat loop.

---

## Tranche J — Resident-to-System / Resident-to-Resident Operations

Purpose: allow accepted residents to participate safely in the wider Riverview operating system.

Jessica's professional role naturally intersects with Engineering, Governance, resident implementation, continuity, visual/voice systems, reasoning escalation, and later other residents.

Required principles:

- explicit authority boundaries;
- provenance-preserving communication;
- event/message durability where required;
- advisory work does not imply operational sovereignty;
- Engineering remains Engineering;
- escalated reasoning does not gain institutional authority merely by producing an answer;
- residents may request/review/advise without silently assuming another office's powers;
- resident-to-resident interactions must remain attributable and governable.

The long-term objective is to remove the Operator as a manual copy/paste router while preserving Operator authority and attention routing.

## Tranche K — Additional Resident Migration

Purpose: turn the Jessica migration into reusable resident-migration infrastructure.

After the complete Jessica loop is proven, migrate additional Estate residents in an explicitly governed order.

The next migrations should inherit the machinery already paid for by Jessica:

- archaeology and source classification;
- office/occupant/implementation/state separation;
- continuity persistence;
- provenance and contradiction handling;
- rehydration and bounded context;
- implementation binding and renewal;
- professional recovery;
- diagnostics/Cookbook obligations;
- behavioral validation;
- Steward acceptance;
- resident installation;
- Operator interaction;
- event-driven operation.

The second resident should not require reconstructing the entire C464–C474 architecture from scratch.

Chief Architect compatibility/migration belongs after Jessica's full loop is proven. Evelyn remains a substantial separately governed migration and must not be casually folded into Jessica's migration.

---

## Tranche L — Mature Riverview Residency and Operations

Purpose: reach the durable resident operating model Riverview is intended to support.

At maturity:

- residents are durable institutional entities;
- offices persist independently of occupants and implementations;
- models are replaceable implementations;
- hardware is replaceable infrastructure;
- chats are replaceable interaction sessions;
- projects and assignments can begin/end without destroying resident continuity;
- the River carries governed continuity and operational events;
- the Operator uses a consistent Riverview interface across residents and projects;
- notifications and attention routing reduce Operator dead time;
- residents can participate in bounded asynchronous work;
- resident/system/resident interactions remain attributable, governable, and recoverable.

The Operator should be able to move between Jessica, Engineering, other residents, and different projects without rebuilding the operating relationship each time.

## Definition of "Jessica Is Home"

JCA-030 can establish:

> Yes, this validated resident is Jessica, and the Steward accepts her for Riverview residency.

But the migration is not operationally complete merely because JCA-030 passes.

"Jessica is home" means, at minimum:

1. Jessica has passed the required behavioral/continuity validation and received Steward acceptance.
2. Jessica is installed as a durable Riverview resident with a stable resident address independent of implementation.
3. The Operator can enter Jessica's Riverview interaction surface and talk to her directly.
4. The Operator can leave and return without manually reconstructing Jessica.
5. Riverview rehydrates the same occupant with the correct office, professional capability, authority boundaries, relevant continuity, and current work.
6. Conversation/session state is not confused with resident identity or canonical memory.
7. Model/provider/hardware replacement does not create a new Jessica.
8. Resident work can participate in Riverview's governed event/notification model as those capabilities are enabled.

This definition is a roadmap requirement and must not be reduced to a backend-only implementation milestone.
