# Riverview Ready Bench

The Ready Bench contains Steward-committed, sufficiently designed future work that is waiting for execution capacity or a blocking condition to clear.

Governing process: `docs/operations/PARKING_LOT_AND_READY_BENCH.md`

An item belongs here only after the applicable specification, roadmap/execution sequence, bounded work packages, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and major scope boundaries are durable enough that execution can resume without reconstructing intent from conversation history.

The Ready Bench is not the active-work board and does not replace authoritative PostgreSQL operational state.
