# PL-0009 — Hermes Liaison Resident or Office

Status: CAPTURED
Classification: Riverview Institutional / Character Architecture
Category: Resident and Office Architecture / Hermes
Priority: Strategic
Originator: Steward
Date Captured: 2026-08-26

## Question

Investigate later whether Riverview should contain a resident or institutional
role responsible for direct human/institutional interaction with the Hermes
capability substrate.

This concept is not Hermes itself becoming a Riverview resident. It is a
possible Riverview role that understands and represents Hermes capabilities to
the institution.

## Possible responsibilities

- explain Hermes activity and provider/model/profile/resource selection;
- expose active workers/sessions appropriately;
- explain learned skills and self-improvement proposals;
- identify whether a requested capability already exists;
- diagnose whether a problem belongs to Riverview, Campaign Runner, Hermes,
  provider, model, or infrastructure;
- translate technical state into institutional/operator language;
- advise bounded model, context, and provider choices.

## Explicit exclusions

The role would not replace Grace, inherit Grace's orchestration authority,
replace Engineering, own Campaign Runner, own institutional acceptance, create
resident canon, or gain authority merely because Hermes can technically perform
an operation.

Future elaboration must determine whether this warrants a new resident/office
or an expansion of an existing Riverview institutional role. Character and
identity decisions remain subject to resident governance and the appropriate
character-architecture authority.

## Promotion condition

Promote only after explicit Steward direction and a bounded authority,
identity, privacy, character, and acceptance specification.
