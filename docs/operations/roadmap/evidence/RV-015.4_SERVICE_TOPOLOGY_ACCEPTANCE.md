# RV-015.4 service topology acceptance

The corrected RV-015.4 scope is software topology and startup, not rack or
cable placement. The bounded topology contract validates unique service
identity, loopback/host ownership, ports, dependencies, startup mechanism,
health mechanism, and data/config ownership. Port collisions and missing
dependencies fail closed under `RIV-INF-0001`.

The current topology records PostgreSQL, River/MCP, Riverview Open WebUI,
Ollama, the explicit llama.cpp GPU provider, and operator/status services from
their accepted configuration and live loopback health boundaries. No physical
layout fact is inferred from this software graph.
