# RV-015.1 criterion audit

Canonical acceptance is: **Every installed device and cable endpoint is labeled
and recorded.**

The machine-discovered host, CPU/RAM, disk, NIC, GPU, USB/HID, and service-port
records satisfy the device inventory portion. The worksheet has no physical
verification for installed cable endpoints, so RV-015.1 cannot be promoted.

The missing facts are the endpoint and destination for each installed cable.
Rack-unit placement is primarily RV-015.2; power/thermal/network health is
RV-015.3; UPS/backup evidence belongs to later infrastructure/recovery rows.
