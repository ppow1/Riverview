## Human paths now available

The Riverview browser operator at `http://127.0.0.1:18082/` now provides a
resident selector sourced from `/api/residents`. Selecting Alfred, Ingrid, or
Helen and submitting a message routes through a resident-bound session. The
browser also exposes a bounded `Create Alfred · Ingrid · Helen room` control and
records turns under the selected admitted speaker. Grace is listed as the
bounded operational participant. Browser session IDs are retained per resident
so switching residents cannot reuse the prior resident's session.

Gateway and browser tests cover Grace, Alfred, Ingrid, and Helen, identity
switching, correlation, provenance, and role-authority separation. They do not
claim that Pat has completed a fresh live resident conversation for each name.

## Grace boundary

`Grace, ...` is recognized conversationally. Ordinary discussion returns an
advisory response. Explicit authorization/work language is marked
`execution_intent_detected` and `execution_requires_seed_promotion`; no
objective or execution is silently created. The existing Steward seed/objective
contract remains the only promotion path.

## Acceptance state

This audit does not promote RV-018.6 or RV-018. It supplies the integrated
resident evidence and leaves final Steward acceptance pending. Remaining
limitations are explicit: fresh human resident-switch acceptance, provider-
generated multi-resident responses, and durable memory publication across
sessions.
